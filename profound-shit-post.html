The Profound Shit Newsletter Philosophy

Itishwhatish

Sample post. A demo of the single-post template.

I want to tell you about a word I made up. It's not a real word, before you go looking it up. It's "Itishwhatish," and yes, I know exactly what it sounds like. It's supposed to sound like that.

It started as a joke, honestly. It is what it is, said fast enough and lazily enough that it turned into one word instead of four. But I kept saying it, about everything, until it stopped being a joke and started being the actual operating system underneath basically every decision I've made since.

Here's the thing nobody tells you about closing a business, or ending a community, or watching something you built stop working the way it used to: you're supposed to feel bad about it. You're supposed to write the LinkedIn post about lessons learned and resilience and growth. I never really felt like doing that. Maadsocial closed and I didn't grieve it, I just noticed it, said Itishwhatish, and started looking at what came next.

You accept the thing completely, no negotiating with it, no story about how it's actually fine, actually good, actually part of the plan. It just is what it is. Then, separately, immediately after, you make the next move anyway.

That's the whole trick, if you can call it a trick. I think people confuse acceptance with giving up. They're not the same thing. Giving up is deciding the next move doesn't matter. Itishwhatish is deciding the last move doesn't get a vote in what happens next.

Fuck it, we ball, basically. Just with better branding.
