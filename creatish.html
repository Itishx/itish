The Current Focus: Braino

Braino is the company in focus right now.Top highlight I am not filling in product claims yet — this space is intentionally reserved for the real context when it is ready. For now, it functions as the clearest signal of the studio: one person building with company-level seriousness, holding the shape open until the idea earns its language.

Everything Built So Far

Every venture, whether it lived or closed, became part of the same operating memory. In order:

  • Braino — the current company in focus. Context coming soon. · In focus, 2026–present
  • Realyst — a LinkedIn alternative built for people, not recruiters. Networking reimagined around authentic work. · Live, 2025–present
  • Levels — a quiet little app for measuring the things that actually move: sleep, focus, pages read. No streaks, no shame. · In beta, 2025–present
  • Writix — writing about love, life, and healing. A creator-led community that grew to 14k followers. · On hold, 2022–2025
  • BrownieBlend — a paid newsletter that extended the Writix world into a more intimate reader community. · Closed, 2024–2025
  • Av8studio — a fuller-scale creative marketing agency, built for brands that wanted more than execution. · On hold
  • Maadsocial — a full-service social media marketing agency, and an early lesson in how service businesses really work. · Closed, 2023–2024
  • Learnor — a learning platform for the curious adult: short courses by people who actually do the thing. · On hold, 2021–2023
