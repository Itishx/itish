The Profound Shit Newsletter

Someone close to me once told me that what I write sounds profound. I didn't argue. The name stuck, and now it's the whole newsletter.

This used to be BrownieBlend, a paid newsletter that grew out of Writix. It lived on Wix, because that's what I had at the time. Wix has limits. I don't really do limits. So BrownieBlend is retired, and this is what replaced it: the same writing, on steroids, living natively on my own site instead of somebody else's template.

It's a blog. It's a newsletter. Honestly it's the same thing, it just depends on whether you're reading this here or it landed in your inbox.
