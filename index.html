§ 02Who Is Itish?
About
About

I make small things, mostly because I cannot stop.

A working biography, revised by whatever gets built next.

I've been building things since I was 18, not because I had a master plan, but because I kept finding problems that felt too interesting to leave alone. I grew a writing community called @writix to 14k followers on Instagram, writing about love, life, and healing.

That turned into BrownieBlend, a paid newsletter. Then came Maadsocial, a marketing agency. Then Av8studio, bigger. Each one taught me something the last one could not.

Now I'm working on Realyst, a LinkedIn alternative built for people, not recruiters, and Sidebrain, a second brain for builders like me.

I do not ship products for the sake of it. I stay with a problem until the right answer starts to feel obvious. If you are reading this, you probably care about building in that same way.

§ 04Working Principles
The recurring themes
Principle01

Build things that feel more human.

The work keeps circling back to the same belief: products should respect attention, reflect real people, and avoid becoming louder than the problem they solve.

People firstProducts
Principle02

Use side projects as a laboratory.

Communities, newsletters, software, and services all teach different lessons. The overlap is where the interesting systems and better instincts start to show up.

ExperimentsPractice
Principle03

Obsess until the solution feels inevitable.

That is the through-line from writing communities to career networks: stay with the problem long enough that the final shape stops feeling optional.

CraftPatience
